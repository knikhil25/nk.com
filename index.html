Who I Am

Curious builder, always making something.

Hey! I'm Nikhil — a student in Cupertino who spends way too much time in the garage soldering things, 3D printing parts, and writing code. My favorite question is "what if I built that myself?"

I love robotics (Arduino + Raspberry Pi are basically my best friends), speed cubing, National Park Junior Ranger badges, and figuring out how everything works under the hood.

I'm a student at Sam H. Lawson Middle School in Cupertino, California — and when I'm not building, I'm usually behind a drum kit or deep in a Math Olympiad problem.

What I've Built

Projects that got out of hand (in the best way).

🤖

Rubik's Cube Solving Robot

A robot that reads a scrambled cube with a webcam using OpenCV and then physically solves it using 28BYJ-48 stepper motors driven by an Arduino Mega. Frame designed in CAD and 3D printed in PLA+.

Arduino Mega OpenCV Python 3D Printing Stepper Motors
🌿

Dracaena Bot / Basil Bot

Science fair project giving a plant "emotions." Uses a capacitive touch sensor and soil moisture sensor to detect interactions, displaying feelings on an LCD. The plant literally tells you when it's thirsty.

Arduino LCD Display Moisture Sensor Capacitive Touch
🥗

AI Calorie Counter

A modern, chat-based calorie tracking app powered by Ollama. Log food naturally, use local vision AI to analyze meal photos, and monitor intake history with interactive charts. Runs entirely on-device for total privacy.

Interactive 3D Solar System

A real-time, interactive 3D solar system built with Three.js. Planets are rendered as hand-crafted clay spheres with unique details — craters on Mercury, volcanoes on Mars, and a raised Great Red Spot on Jupiter. On load, all planets are positioned accurately for that day.
